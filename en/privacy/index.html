Never Run Straight Privacy Policy

Singularity published this policy on 27 November 2026. It applies to our official website and support service from that date. It applies to our first public iOS release on the Korean App Store from the day we distribute it. Private test builds are covered by the notice provided for their test.

Children and parents · Recipients, overseas processing and your choices · Retention, deletion and restoration · Rights, security and complaints

Before you play

  • Basic play. We check your year of birth on your device. If your age is unknown or you decline optional features, you can still play and save progress on the device.
  • Optional features. You can choose rewarded ads and personalised ads separately. Purchases use Apple’s store. We do not send an ad age flag or request an ad before giving the relevant notice and obtaining the necessary consent. The first iOS release does not send sign-in or transaction events for Unity IAP’s own purposes. You do not need to accept ads to buy an item.
  • Analytics and diagnostics. The first public iOS release does not send optional gameplay analytics or optional Unity Engine crash and performance diagnostics. We describe the server records used to verify purchases and prevent duplicate grants below.
  • Your data. You can change privacy choices or request account and data deletion in Settings. If you have uninstalled the app, use our account deletion page.

Children and parents

We ask for a birth year, not a birthday or name. The year stays on the device. Because we do not know your birthday, we also apply child settings if you turn 14 during the current year. Child settings disable personalised ads, cross-app tracking prompts and promotional notifications. Players whose age is unknown and players in child settings can play battles, make progress on the device and use items they already have. In the first iOS release, optional ads and new purchases are unavailable to this group while we have no procedure to verify guardian consent. Support accepts requests for restoration of existing purchases and deletion.

Where Korean law requires consent for a feature to process personal information belonging to a child under 14, we must verify consent given by a parent or legal guardian who has been told what the feature does. We keep that feature closed to children if we do not provide this procedure. Entering a birth year, a child tapping a confirmation button or Apple approving a family purchase does not provide this consent. Before verification, we do not initiate consent-dependent transfers such as a rewarded-ad age signal, ad preloading or optional purchasing-service sessions and automatic order lookups. A guardian may ask us to access, delete or stop processing their child’s information through game support. Korean Personal Information Protection Act, Article 22-2

Accounts, saves and purchases

When you use online features, we use an anonymous Player ID, sign-in information stored on your device, progress and stage records, currency, gear, settings, ad rewards and counts, records of purchases or refunds already processed and save numbers and timestamps. Online authentication uses access times, IP addresses and device or installation identifiers. The first public version does not offer Game Center linking. We keep the information needed to sync and restore game progress until account deletion. Your birth year and privacy settings stay on the device.

Apple handles payment details. We do not receive your card number. Our server verifies Apple-signed transactions to grant, refund or reclaim items and prevent duplicate grants. It does not retain the original signed transaction. Records include the Store transaction ID, a one-way hash of the original transaction ID, product, amount and currency, platform and live or test status, purchase, grant, refund and transfer times and status, refund reversals, amounts reclaimed, archival time and the Player ID used for the grant. Operational settings also allow new orders to be paused during an outage and protect cloud saves.

Article 15(1)(4) of the Korean Personal Information Protection Act covers processing needed to authenticate an online account, save progress, and fulfil or restore a requested purchase. Article 15(1)(2) and Article 6 of Korea’s e-commerce consumer protection law cover statutory transaction records. Declining optional ads or analytics does not remove existing items or purchase rights. If you decline ads, you can still ask support about ownership, restoration or deletion. In-game receipt verification requires a connection and the Apple account used for the purchase.

Retention, deletion and restoration

  • We keep contracts, cancellations, payment and delivery records for five years from the last relevant record for the transaction, consumer complaints and dispute records for three years and display or advertising records for six months. Korean e-commerce consumer protection enforcement decree, Article 6
  • At account deletion we remove the account identifier from the transaction record and place statutory records in separate storage. A one-way hash and marker remain in the grant ledger to prevent a repeat grant of the same transaction. We check daily for archived records whose retention period has expired. Other information is securely deleted without delay once its purpose is fulfilled or its period ends.
  • If a guardian asks to cancel a minor’s purchase, we may request only the information needed to verify age and the relationship. Please redact resident registration numbers and unnecessary addresses. We immediately destroy verification documents after verification and retain only the request date and outcome for the applicable period.
  • If you restore purchases to a new game account after deletion using the same Apple account, we restore ongoing entitlements Apple confirms, including Starter Pack gear, Growth Pass or Loop Effect gear. Gems, gold or scrolls granted once with that product are not granted a second time during restoration, whether they were spent or remain unused. We do not reconnect the deleted account’s archived record to the new account.

Rights, security and complaints

You or your legal guardian may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or withdrawal of consent. Withdrawal preserves existing purchase rights and basic gameplay. We may ask for the minimum information needed to verify identity or authority. As a rule, we inform you within ten days of the action taken or the legal reason for any limitation. We explain why a statutory transaction record needs to be kept during its retention period.

  • Deletion in the game. In lobby Settings use Privacy & Account → Delete Account & Data. Reset Save Data resets game progress only. Account deletion cannot finish while the server is unreachable. If interrupted, reconnect and retry. After server game data is removed, local progress is reset. If erasure of previous analytics or the same account’s sign-in information is still pending, the Game retries after reconnection. You can also request deletion without the app.
  • Privacy enquiries and complaints. Contact Singularity’s privacy and support function at sglrt77@gmail.com or follow our support instructions. Include your Player ID if you know it. You can make a request without it. In Korea you can also contact the Personal Information Infringement Report Center or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee.

We restrict administrative access, use two-step verification, encrypt communications, keep server access records and store secret keys separately. We do not make decisions with a significant effect on your rights or obligations by automated means alone. We announce policy changes on the site and in the app at least seven days in advance, or 30 days in advance for changes that materially affect your rights.
