Delete your account and game data

You can delete your Singularity Labs game account in the app or request deletion by email.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Connect to the internet. From the lobby, open Settings → Privacy & Account.
  2. Select Delete Account & Data.
  3. Review what will be deleted, then select Yes. Deleted progress and in-game currency cannot be recovered.

If deletion fails, check your connection and try again from the same menu.

Request deletion without the app

Email sglrt77@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion request”. Include your Player ID if you know it. In the app, you can find it under Settings → Privacy & Account → Copy Player ID.

You can send a request even if you do not know your Player ID or have uninstalled the app. Our support team will verify which account to delete and your authority to request deletion, then email you the outcome.

What happens to my data?

Deletion covers your game progress, in-game currency and equipment. Once the server confirms that your game data has been deleted, the app clears the progress saved on your device. If a request to delete play statistics or the sign-in account is still pending, the app retries when it next connects to that account. You can ask us by email about a pending request.

Purchase records are kept separately after their link to your Player ID is removed. Your device keeps your choices to opt out of play statistics and personalized ads.
