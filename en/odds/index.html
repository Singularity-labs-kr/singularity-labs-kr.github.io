Never Run Straight

Chest drop rates

Game version 1.0.0 · Rates verified November 27, 2026

The Game has two random items (loot boxes): the Royal Armory Chest and the Armory Chest (opened with gold). The same information is shown in the Game on the chest purchase screen, the odds screen and the chest result screen. We announce any change to the odds in the Game and on this page before it takes effect.

  • Odds below are per single opening, shown as percentages. Individual item rates use four decimal places, with extra digits below 1% as needed to retain four significant digits. Rounding can make the displayed total differ slightly from 100%.
  • "Loop Effect" items go in the weapon slot.
  • Items with fixed contents (gem packs, starter pack, growth pass, Loop Effect Packs) and equipment upgrades and merges are not random items. Upgrades and merges always give a fixed result.

Royal Armory Chest

  • Cost: 180 gems per opening. There is no discount for multiple openings.
  • Bonus: 1 scroll with every opening (fixed amount).
  • Squire-grade equipment does not appear.

Guarantee

  • If 11 openings in a row give no Royal-or-higher item, the 12th opening always gives a Royal-or-higher item.
  • On the guaranteed 12th opening, the grade odds are Royal 90.9091% and Founder 9.0909% (keeping the 10:1 ratio).

  • If the guarantee gives a Royal item, you choose which of the 10 body-slot equipment items you receive. Loop Effects cannot be chosen.
  • If the guarantee gives a Founder item, the item is decided at random in the same proportions as the Founder column in the regular item odds table.
  • The count restarts whenever you get a Royal-or-higher item, guaranteed or not. The count is kept when you close the app or the day changes. The chest card in the shop shows how many opens are left until a Royal-or-higher item is guaranteed.

Armory Chest (gold)

  • Cost: 600 gold per opening. There is no discount for multiple openings.
  • You can also open this chest as the day-7 attendance reward or by watching a rewarded ad when you have an ad claim available. Each method uses the same odds.
  • Bonus: 1 scroll with every opening (fixed amount).
  • No guarantee.
