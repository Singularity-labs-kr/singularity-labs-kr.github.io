Refund Policy — Never Run Straight

Effective date: 27 November 2026

This policy explains sections 9 and 11 of our Terms of Service in detail. If they conflict, the Terms of Service prevail. This English version is a translation of the Korean policy.

1. The basics

  • Paid items are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Both payment and refunds go through the Store.
  • So refund requests generally go to the Store. We explain the Store's process, and where we can process a refund ourselves, we do.
  • We never receive your card number or other payment details.
  • Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the consumer protection laws of the country where you live.

2. Cancelling within 7 days

Within 7 days of the day the item reaches your game account (or of the day you received the contract details on the purchase screen or receipt, if later), you can cancel and be refunded within the limits below.

Item What you can cancel
Gem packs The gems you have not used. Free gems are treated as spent first, and purchased gems in the order you bought them. So you can cancel the gems from that pack that are still left. The refund is the amount paid × (gems left ÷ gems the pack gave you), rounded up to the nearest won (or cent).
Starter pack, Loop Effect Packs The whole bundle, if you have used nothing in it: gems are judged as above, equipment has not been equipped, upgraded or merged, and you still have at least the gold and scrolls it gave you. If you used part of the bundle, you can still request cancellation for separately deliverable parts whose supply has not begun. We check the delivery and use of each component and explain the basis and amount of the refund.
Growth pass If you have not claimed any pass reward. If you claimed some rewards, you can still request cancellation of any divisible part whose supply has not begun. We review claimed rewards separately from future rewards.
  • The regular Armory Chest opens with gold. The Royal Armory Chest opens with gems. Neither chest is sold separately in the Store. Gems used to open a Royal Armory Chest count as spent.
  • "Reset Save Data" in Settings keeps your unused purchased gems, so what you can cancel for gem packs stays the same. Gold and scrolls from bundles are erased by a reset and do not come back (gear returns with your purchase rights). Rewards claimed from the Growth Pass's paid track cannot be claimed again either. So check whether you want to cancel a purchase before resetting. Even after a reset, we handle a cancellation request within 7 days using the purchase records.

3. When cancellation is limited

Cancellation is limited for gems you have already used and items you have already used (anything beyond the limits above), or once the period in section 2 has expired. Any different starting date or period required by law applies. We show this on the purchase screen in advance. These limits apply only if we have provided the notices and any trial or other measures required by law.

However, if an item differs from its description, advertising or contract terms, you may cancel within 3 months of receiving it and within 30 days of when you learned (or could have learned) of the difference, whether or not you used it.

4. How to request a refund

Purchases on iPhone or iPad (App Store)

  • Ask Apple directly at Report a Problem, signing in with your Apple Account and choosing the purchase.
  • Apple reviews App Store refund requests and returns approved payments to the original payment method. Using the Store process preserves your statutory rights.
  • If Apple declines but you believe sections 2 or 3 apply, contact us and we will explain what we can do.

Purchases on Android (Google Play)

  • You can ask Google for a refund from your order history in the Google Play app or website.
  • You can also ask us. If sections 2 or 3 apply, we will refund you through Google Play.

What to include when you contact us

Use the email address on our Support page and include the following.

  1. your in-game Player ID (Settings in the lobby → Privacy & Account → Copy Player ID);
  2. the Store order number (the "Order ID" in Apple's receipt email, or the number starting with "GPA." in Google's receipt email). For a payment the Game said was "processed for an earlier game account", tap it under Settings → Privacy & Account → Purchase records to copy its number. For Apple payments this copies the App Store transaction ID, which may differ from the Order ID in the receipt email; either is fine;
  3. the purchase date and item name; and
  4. what you are asking for (cancellation, a minor's purchase, a payment error, etc.).

We reply within 3 business days. For a digital-content withdrawal under Korean e-commerce law, we fulfil the applicable refund obligation within 3 business days of withdrawal. For card payments and similar methods, we promptly request suspension or cancellation of the charge as required by law. The date a credit appears on your statement depends on the Store and your payment method.

5. Purchases by minors

  • In Korea, a purchase made by a person under 19 without a parent's or guardian's consent can be cancelled by that person or the guardian, except for purchases within money the guardian allowed the minor to spend, or where the minor used deception. Other countries have their own rules.
  • Send App Store requests to Apple, and Google Play requests to Google or to us. When you ask us, include the details in section 4 and the minimum information needed to confirm you are the guardian. Please hide anything we do not need. We delete it as soon as we have confirmed.

6. What happens after a refund

  • When a purchase is cancelled or refunded for any reason, including a refund granted by the Store, we may remove the gems and items delivered by that purchase. We only remove what that game account actually received.
  • Gems removed: for a gem pack, the gems in that pack; for the starter pack and Loop Effect Packs, the gems in the pack; for the Growth Pass, the gems that account has already claimed from paid rewards.
  • Gems already spent are deducted from your gem balance, and any shortfall is covered by gems you earn later. Your balance is never shown below zero.
  • If a Loop Effect Pack is refunded, we remove that Loop Effect gear (even if you upgraded it by merging). If the Growth Pass is refunded, you can no longer claim its future paid rewards. A refunded starter pack cannot be bought again.
  • Partial refunds: if the Store refunds only part of a payment and tells us the percentage (App Store), we remove only that percentage of the gems (rounding any fraction up to one gem) and leave rights such as gear and the Growth Pass in place. If the refunded percentage later increases, we remove only the additional share; if it becomes a full refund, we also remove the rights. A refund for which the Store gives no percentage is treated as a full refund. If you actually got only part of your money back, contact us as in section 4 and we will correct it.
  • If another game account received the rights from the same payment (for example, through "Restore Purchases"), we remove what that account received on the same basis.
  • When we remove anything, the Game tells you. If gems are removed, it says how many; if only an item such as gear or the Growth Pass is removed, it says "A payment was refunded. The purchased item was removed."
  • If the Store reverses a refund: we give back the gems and items we removed. If the removal left a gem debt, we clear that debt first and give back the remaining gems. Loop Effect gear comes back at the grade and level it had when you first received it. Anything you added before the refund by merging or upgrading it (the higher grade and level, and the currency you spent on them) does not come back. If you have questions about this, contact us as described in section 4.

7. Payment errors and delayed delivery

  • If you see "Payment complete. Your items are on the way" (or similar) after paying, reopen the Game while connected to the internet. We do not finalise a payment with the Store until delivery is confirmed, so Google automatically refunds a Google Play payment that is not confirmed within 3 days.
  • We do not accept payments while the Game cannot sign in to your game account (for example, when offline).
  • If, after reinstalling, the Game says a payment "was processed for an earlier game account", the gems from that payment do not carry over to your current game account. If you never received those gems, copy its number from Purchase records and contact us with the details in section 4. We will check its delivery status and let you know, and a payment you did not receive is handled as described in the next item.
  • If you were charged twice or did not receive an item you paid for, contact us with the details in section 4. If you have deleted your account and can no longer see your Player ID, the order number is enough. We will check the delivery status, let you know, and help you get a refund through the Store.

8. If the Game ends

  • We will announce the end of service at least 30 days in advance and stop selling paid items from that announcement.
  • We will refund unused purchased gems. The value of one gem is based on the price you paid, excluding bonus gems, and if you bought gems several times, the most recently bought gems are treated as the ones remaining.
  • Unused items bought within 7 days before the end date are refunded in full under section 2.
  • We accept refund requests for at least 30 days after the end date and explain how in the announcement.
  • Gems, gold and items obtained for free are not refunded.
